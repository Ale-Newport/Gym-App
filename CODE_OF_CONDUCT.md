# Code of conduct

Forge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## The short version

We want this to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully,
and focus on what is best for the people who use the app.

**Do not:** use sexualised language or imagery, troll, insult or make personal or political attacks,
harass anyone publicly or privately, publish someone's private information without their explicit
permission, or behave in any way that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Fitness and nutrition are personal subjects. Never comment on anyone's body, weight or diet.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews — and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@Ale-Newport](https://github.com/Ale-Newport), through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Ale-Newport/Gym-App/security/advisories/new) (it is
the one private channel GitHub offers on this repository) — write "Code of conduct" in the title.
Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is
inappropriate, following the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
